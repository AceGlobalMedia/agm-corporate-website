The Process

AGM doesn’t begin with technology. AGM begins with the business.

Our methodology is the product: nine steps from how a business works today to a simpler, automated operation.

DISCOVER→OBSERVE→MAP→IDENTIFY→DESIGN→BUILD→TEST→IMPLEMENT→OPTIMIZE

We don’t automate broken processes. We understand them first.

Most technology projects start with a solution: “we need an app,” “we need a new website.” The result is often a faster version of the same problems.

Technology doesn’t appear until step six. The first five steps happen inside your business.

The nine steps

Nine steps. One practical question at each.

01

DISCOVER

“What is this business trying to achieve — and what is getting in the way?”

  • Leadership and stakeholder interviews
  • Goals, growth plans and constraints
  • Current systems, data and pain points

Result: A shared understanding of the business and what success looks like.

02

OBSERVE

“What really happens on a busy Tuesday afternoon?”

  • Time on the floor with the people doing the work
  • Workarounds, side-spreadsheets and chat threads
  • Where people wait, re-type or chase

Result: The real operation, including the parts nobody documented.

03

MAP

“Where does each piece of information start, travel and end?”

  • End-to-end workflows by department
  • Handoffs, approvals and exceptions
  • Data movement between people and systems

Result: A complete map everyone in the business recognises.

04

IDENTIFY

“What should be removed, simplified, connected or automated?”

  • Bottlenecks and delays
  • Duplicate entry and rework
  • Risk, error and control gaps

Result: Problems and opportunities, prioritised by impact.

05

DESIGN

“What should the operation look like — and only then, what does it need?”

  • Redesigned future-state workflow
  • Roles, permissions and responsibilities
  • Phased roadmap and scope

Result: An approved future-state operation and technology blueprint.

06

BUILD

“What is the right tool for each part of the improved workflow?”

  • Custom systems and portals
  • Web and mobile experiences
  • Dashboards, integrations and automation

Result: Working technology shaped around the redesigned operation.

07

TEST

“Does it hold up on the busiest, messiest day?”

  • Scenario testing with real staff
  • Edge cases and exceptions from the map
  • Permissions and data accuracy checks

Result: A system proven against real work, not ideal conditions.

08

IMPLEMENT

“How do we change the way people work without disrupting the business?”

  • Configuration and data migration
  • Role-based staff training
  • Phased rollout with go-live support

Result: The business running on the new operation, with staff confident using it.

09

OPTIMIZE

“What have we learned — and what should improve next?”

  • Usage and operational KPI tracking
  • Continuous refinements
  • New phases as the business grows

Result: An operation that keeps getting better after launch.

Why it matters

A different starting point produces a different result.

Technology-first projects

  • Start with “we need an app”
  • Built around how the process is supposed to work
  • Exceptions discovered after launch
  • Project ends at launch

The AGM Process

  • Start with how the business operates
  • Built around how the work is actually done
  • Exceptions mapped before design
  • Ongoing optimization as the business evolves
