What we build

Technology chosen by the operation, not the other way around.

Every AGM solution is the output of The Process. These are the tools we use — alone or combined — once we understand what the business actually needs.

Core practice

Custom Business Systems

Purpose-built systems that run the day-to-day operation — designed around your redesigned workflow, your roles and your exceptions.

Internal operating systems

The central system your team runs the business on — orders, jobs, inventory, billing.

Customer & staff portals

Self-service for customers; role-based tools and permissions for staff.

Workflow automation

Approvals, notifications, follow-ups and handoffs that happen without chasing.

Dashboards & reporting

Real-time visibility for managers, built from the data the operation already creates.

Integrations

Connecting accounting, payments, messaging and existing tools so data moves once.

AI where it earns its place

Classification, drafting, extraction and assistance — applied to specific mapped steps.

Delivery capability

Web & App Development

When a website or application is part of the solution, we build it — connected to the operation behind it, not as a standalone brochure.

How it differs

  • Websites that feed bookings, enquiries and orders directly into your system
  • Mobile experiences for drivers, field staff and customers
  • Apps scoped from the workflow map, not a feature wishlist
  • Built to integrate with the rest of your operation

If you already know you need an app, we’ll still start by asking why.

Sometimes the answer is an app. Sometimes it’s an integration, a portal or a simpler workflow. Discovery tells us which.
