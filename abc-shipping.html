Proof case · Logistics

ABC Shipping Services: we didn’t build an app. We rebuilt how the operation runs.

ABC Shipping is the clearest demonstration of the AGM methodology.

Discover · Observe · Map

We followed every package, invoice and driver through the business.

Discover

What management needed: visibility, faster collections, fewer errors, and room to scale without more manual work.

Observe

Time with warehouse staff, front counter, billing and drivers — capturing how work actually moved, workarounds included.

Map

End-to-end: receiving → processing → invoicing → customer notice → collection → delivery → reporting, with every exception.

Identify · Design · Build

Every operational area. One connected system.

Warehouse receiving× Packages logged manually on arrival→ Scan-based receiving tied to the customer account
Package processing× Status tracked in notes and memory→ Every package has a live status and history
Invoices & billing× Invoices re-keyed from package records→ Invoices generated from processed packages
Customer communication× Staff calling and messaging one by one→ Automatic notifications at key milestones
Collections× Outstanding balances chased by hand→ Balances visible, reminders automated
Deliveries & drivers× Routes and proof of delivery on paper→ Driver workflow with delivery confirmation
Staff permissions× Everyone sees everything, or nothing→ Role-based access by job function
Exceptions× Problems handled ad hoc→ Defined exception paths with accountability
Reporting× Numbers assembled at month end→ Management dashboards in real time

Illustrative summary of the before/after operating model.

Test · Implement · Optimize

Proven with the people who use it.

Tested on real receiving days, billing runs and delivery routes. Rolled out with training by role. Refined as the operation evolves.
